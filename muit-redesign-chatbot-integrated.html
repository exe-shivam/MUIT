About MUIT

Inspired by a vision. Rooted in nature.


Inspired by the vision of Maharishi Mahesh Yogi Ji, MUIT is a leading private university in Uttar Pradesh. It was established under the MUIT Uttar Pradesh Act No. 31 of 2001 and offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and doctoral programs.

The university was established in the lap of nature. Its lush-green campus supports academic, cultural and sports activities, and student centricity is the core value behind every curricular, co-curricular and extra-curricular activity.
